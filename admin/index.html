Quick help

Adding a car Tap the gold “Add a car” button, take or choose your photos, type the number plate and hit Look up. Fill in price and mileage, then Publish.
Marking a car sold Tap the car in your list, then “Mark as sold”. It comes off the stock page straight away and shows under Recently Sold with no price.
Photos not uploading? Usually a weak signal. They’ll go up fine on wi-fi. Nothing is lost, so save it as a draft and add the photos later.
