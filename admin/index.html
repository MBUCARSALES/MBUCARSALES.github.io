Quick help

Adding a car Tap the gold “Add a car” button, take or choose your photos, type the number plate and hit Look up. Fill in price and mileage, then Publish.
Marking a car sold Tap the car in your list, then “Mark as sold”. It moves off the stock page straight away and appears under Recently Sold — without the price.
Photos not uploading? Usually a weak signal. They’ll upload fine on wi-fi. Nothing is lost — save it as a draft and add the photos later.
