Make the app icons

This page creates the three icon files the admin app needs for your dad’s iPhone home screen. You only ever need to do this once.

Downloaded. Now move the three files into the admin folder.

Sharing image

This is what shows when someone shares your homepage on WhatsApp or Facebook. (Individual cars use their own photo automatically.)

Downloaded. Put it in assets/img/.

Better still: once you have a good photo of your best car, replace this file with it. Save it as og-default.jpg, 1200 × 630 pixels.

What to do with them

  1. Click the button above — three PNG files land in your Downloads.
  2. Move icon-180.png, icon-192.png and icon-512.png into the admin folder, next to index.html.
  3. Commit and push. Done — the home screen icon will look proper.

If you skip this the app still works perfectly, the home screen icon just won’t be branded.
