Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you give us. Last updated .

MBU Car Sales (“we”, “us”) is the data controller for the personal information collected through this website. This page explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are under UK data protection law.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through the forms on this site:

  • Your name, phone number and (optionally) email address
  • The content of any message or enquiry you send us
  • If you are selling a vehicle: its registration, make, model, mileage, condition and history
  • If you ask us to find you a car: what you are looking for and your budget

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics that identify you personally.

How we measure interest in our cars

We count how many times each car is looked at, how often the photos are opened, and how often someone taps to call or message us. This tells us which cars people like and which are priced wrongly. It is ordinary business sense, not surveillance.

Deliberately, this counting stores nothing at all on your device — no cookies, no local storage, no fingerprint — and records no IP addresses. A random, throwaway reference exists only while your browser tab is open, purely so that refreshing a page is not counted ten times. It is discarded the moment you close the tab and cannot be linked to you, your device, or any previous visit.

Because nothing is stored on your device and nothing identifies you, this needs no cookie banner and involves no personal data. That is why you have not been asked to consent to anything.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange a viewing, provide a valuation, or complete a sale. That is it. The lawful basis is your consent when you submit a form, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact our business.

Who else sees it

Enquiries are stored securely in a database hosted by Supabase and, where you have given us an email address, may be forwarded to our own business inbox. Photographs on this website are hosted by Cloudinary. These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone else for marketing. If a sale proceeds, we may need to share your details with the DVLA to transfer the V5C, and with a finance provider only if you specifically ask us to arrange finance.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a sale are deleted within 12 months. Records relating to an actual vehicle sale are kept for six years, as we are required to for tax and consumer protection purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these, just email us at or ring and we will sort it out.

Cookies

This website does not set any tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser may store small amounts of data locally so the site works properly, but nothing that identifies you and nothing that is sent to third parties for profiling.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Questions about anything on this page? Get in touch — we would rather explain it properly than have you wondering.
