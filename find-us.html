How to find us

We are set back off Westgate Road, so you will not spot us from the car. The easy way is to aim for the Gulf petrol station and take the lane immediately after it. Photos of every turn are below.

Look for the Gulf petrol station We are directly behind it. If you reach the petrol station you have arrived; the entrance is the lane running up its right-hand side as you face it from Westgate Road.

Step by step

Get onto Westgate Road

We are on the stretch between Newcastle city centre and Gosforth. Coming out of town, we are on your left; coming the other way, on your right. Put NE4 9PQ into your sat nav to get onto the right stretch of road.

Find the Gulf petrol station

This is the landmark to aim for. It sits right on Westgate Road and is easy to see. We are tucked in behind it, which is why the postcode alone will not put you at our gate.

Take the lane beside it

Immediately past the petrol station there is a lane between the buildings, with green metal gates. That is the entrance. Drive up it, past the garages, and the lot opens out on your left.

You have arrived

Our cars are parked in the yard at the top. If the gate is closed or you cannot see us, give us a ring and one of us will come out and wave you in.

What it looks like on the day

Coming to see a particular car? Give us a ring or send a message before you set off. We will have it out, warmed up and ready rather than parked in behind three others, and we will make sure somebody is here to meet you.

Address

The postcode gets you to the right stretch of Westgate Road. The last hundred yards are easier with the photos above.

Lost? Just ring us

Honestly, it is easier than it sounds once you have been once. If you are on Westgate Road and cannot see the lane, call and we will talk you in.
